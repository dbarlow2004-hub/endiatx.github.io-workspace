📲 Phone notifications

Reading reminders arrive as notifications through the free ntfy app, the same one the daily news brief uses.

  1. Install ntfy from the App Store or Google Play and allow notifications.
  2. In the app tap + and subscribe to a hard-to-guess topic name, e.g. . You can use the same topic as your news brief.
  3. Enter that topic here and tap Save, then send a test.
