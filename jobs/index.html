02Five tracks, ranked

Apply across at least three tracks in parallel — coordinator-level NYC roles move in 2–3 week cycles, so parallel beats sequential.

Track A — Green building & sustainability consulting Best long-term fit

~$61k–90k coordinator · analyst roles higher

NYC is the U.S. capital of building decarbonization (Local Law 97 compliance is a hiring engine). Your degree + LEED GA-in-progress + hands-on construction literacy is exactly the entry profile these firms want.

  • Steven Winter Associates — NYC-based sustainability/building-performance consultancy, hires entry analysts year-round. Flagship target.
  • Bright Power — NYC energy & sustainability for real estate; mission-driven, hires early-career.
  • Buro Happold — global engineering consultancy with a large NYC office and a sustainability advisory analyst pipeline.
  • NYC government (cityjobs.nyc.gov) — search "sustainability": Analyst–Sustainability Policy, Climate & Sustainability Planner, and DDC Sustainability Coordinator postings recur regularly. Slower cycles, so apply early.
  • Also watch: WSP, Tishman Speyer sustainability team, JLL/CBRE ESG groups.

Track B — Startup operations (climate & healthtech) Fastest to land

~$45k–70k · avg ~$29/hr for ops associates in NYC

Ops associate / ops coordinator at an early-stage startup is the straightest line from what you do at ENDIATX today. Climate-tech startups double the mission fit; NYC has hundreds hiring.

  • Built In NYC — entry-level ops — the single best board for this track; new postings daily.
  • ClimateTechList — New York — 9,000+ climate roles refreshed daily; filter to NYC + operations.
  • Indeed — climate startup NYC — ~240 active climate-tech postings.
  • Your cover-note hook: "I currently run ops at a medical robotics startup — buildouts, vendors, inventory, and an FDA submission timeline."

Track C — Workplace experience & office buildout Direct experience match

CBRE Workplace Experience Coordinator: $60k–65k · Industrious ~$25/hr

You literally transformed an office and warehouse into an investor-ready space — that's this entire job category. Steady demand, quick hiring cycles, and a foot in the door at major real-estate firms with sustainability teams you can move into later.

  • CBRE — Workplace Experience Coordinator roles post continuously in NYC at $60–65k; search CBRE NYC on Glassdoor.
  • Industrious — Member Experience Associate, NYC locations (~$25/hr); also Convene and JLL equivalents.
  • Pitch the ENDIATX buildout with photos — your portfolio's conference-room project is the interview closer here.

Track D — Medical device & regulatory project coordination

Entry regulatory affairs in NY: roughly $46–72/hr equivalent ranges · 27+ entry postings on Glassdoor

Hands-on 510(k) project management as a new grad is genuinely rare. NYC healthtech is big enough to reward it — regulatory affairs associate, clinical project coordinator, or ops at a device startup.

  • Built In NYC — healthtech — filter to operations / project roles.
  • Glassdoor — entry regulatory affairs NYC — associate roles that "assist in drafting and submitting 510(k)s" describe what you already do.
  • Ask ENDIATX leadership for intros — medtech is a warm-referral industry and your regulatory consultants know NYC people.

Track E — Fabrication, model shops & exhibition design Highest pay ceiling

Design fabrication in Brooklyn: avg ~$92k ($81k–100k)

Laser cutting, 3D printing, SolidWorks, silicone molds, finish carpentry — NYC's architecture model shops and Brooklyn fabrication studios pay real money for exactly this. Smaller market, but you're unusually qualified.

  • Glassdoor — architectural model maker NYC — ~18 active postings (Aug 2026); firms want CNC/laser + physical craft.
  • ZipRecruiter — design fabrication Brooklyn — studios in Long Island City & Brooklyn hiring now.
  • Whitney Museum — Exhibition Design internship for Fall 2026 (scaled models, presentation materials) — a paid-portfolio route if you want a design-school launchpad.

~$57k–63k for bilingual coordinator roles
