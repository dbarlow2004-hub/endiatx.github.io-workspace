39 NYC companies worth a direct email — with the emails written.

No job boards, no LinkedIn. These are real shops, studios, startups, and orgs found by researching who actually does the work you're good at — most within blocks of each other in the Brooklyn Navy Yard, Red Hook, and LIC. Each card has the public email and phone found for them, plus a personalized draft: hit ✉ Open in Gmail and the email is sitting in your compose window ready to send.

How to run this: 5 emails a day, top of each category first — personalize one sentence if you can. Attach your resume PDF before sending (Gmail links can't pre-attach). No reply in 3–4 business days → call the number on the card (script at the bottom). ⚠ Emails were collected from public listings — if one bounces, use the contact page linked on the card and send it there instead.

Phone script (for the follow-up call)

30 seconds, standing up, smiling — it comes through.

"Hi, my name is Barlow Clark-Schoeb — I emailed a few days ago and wanted to put a voice to it. I'm finishing up at a medical robotics startup in the Bay Area where I run operations and build prototypes, and I'm moving to New York in September. I'm reaching out because I really admire what [company] does, and I'd love fifteen minutes with whoever runs [the shop / projects / programs] — even if you're not hiring right now. Is there someone I could speak with, or an email I should send my portfolio to directly?"

If they say "we're not hiring": "Totally understand — I'd still love to be on your radar for freelance or overhire work this fall. Who's the right person to keep in touch with?" (Fabrication shops especially staff project-to-project — "overhire" is the magic word.)
