What I do best
The draw is my craft
Possession wins lacrosse games, and the draw is where possession starts. It's the part of my game I've built my career on — and where I can make an immediate difference for a program in 2027.

I finished 2026 with 72 draw controls — roughly a third of UConn's entire team total — starting all 17 games, leading the Huskies in draws, and earning All-Big East Second Team and Big East All-Tournament honors (top-3 in the conference in draw controls).

I direct the draw — I don't just collect it

A statistical "draw control" is credited to whoever gains possession, so when I win the draw and place it to a circle teammate, she gets the stat. That means my true draw-winning impact runs higher than my individual number — and it lets a coach design draw plays around wings and fast-break transition, not just self-collection.
