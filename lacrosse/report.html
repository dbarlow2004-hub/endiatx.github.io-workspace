03 Transfer-portal & 5th-year action plan

✓ Grad-transfer status = maximum flexibility

Windows are an undergraduate mechanism. Graduate transfers in women's lacrosse may be entered into the portal at any time, are immediately eligible with no waiver and no year in residence, and multiple transfers no longer trigger a sit-out year (post-2024 settlement).

Exact process — how you enter

  1. Confirm degree + academic standing. Immediate eligibility depends on completing your UConn degree and leaving academically eligible.
  2. Complete the NCAA Transfer Educational Module (required video) and save the completion certificate.
  3. Email UConn compliance a written "Notification of Transfer" request — your name, sport (women's lacrosse), student ID, date, and clear intent to enter the portal. Ask them to confirm receipt.
  4. UConn has 2 business days to enter you into the portal after that written notice. They cannot refuse or stall past it. Follow up on day 2 if needed.
  5. Get the portal confirmation email. Leave the "do-not-contact" flag OFF so coaches can reach you; add your contact info.
  6. Now coaches may contact you — recruiting conversations become permissible only at this point.
  7. Get any grad-year athletic aid offer in writing (amount, assistantship/merit aid). Women's lacrosse is an equivalency sport — awards can be full, partial, or walk-on; the House-settlement roster limit is 38 with more scholarships now fundable.
  8. Enroll full-time in the new school's grad program → immediately eligible for spring 2027.
✗ Tampering — protect yourself

Coaches at other schools cannot contact you about transferring until you are in the portal — that's tampering (penalties got harsher in 2026). You initiating a hello isn't itself a violation, but a coach who engages on transfer specifics pre-portal is exposed. Clean path: enter the portal first, then talk. The proposed pre-portal "engagement period" (Prop. 2026-39) is not adopted — don't rely on it.
