How this tool works

This is a decision-support and tracking desk for a women's lacrosse draw specialist exploring a graduate-transfer year in 2026-27. It does two things: (1) helps you shortlist D1 programs, and (2) cross-references them against graduate degrees that fit your career goals.

The "Match" score

Each card gets a 0-100 score computed live from three weighted inputs you control in the sidebar:

  • Draw-role opportunity — a transparent proxy: lower-tier / rebuilding programs generally offer a clearer path to earning draw reps as a transfer, while blue-bloods with established draw stars are harder to crack. This is an opportunity estimate, not a confirmed roster hole.
  • Grad-degree fit — how many of your selected target degrees the school is tagged as offering.
  • Program level fit — rewards schools matching the level you picked (top programs, no preference, or developing programs where playing time is likelier).

What the data is (and isn't)

  • The program list, conferences, regions and degree tags are hand-curated from general knowledge of the D1 landscape. Conference realignment and roster turnover happen constantly — verify.
  • Degree tags are indicative ("likely offers, verify"). Use the ◎ Degrees link on each card to confirm exact programs, admissions requirements and deadlines.
  • There is no live draw-control feed. To confirm real need, use the ◎ Roster and ◎ Portal links and check who's graduating and current draw-control stats on NCAA.com.
